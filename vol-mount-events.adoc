[.lead]
vol.mount events are reported when a volume or aggregate is mounted as read-only, and corrective actions typically involve following the documented corrective action guidance.

== vol.mount.readOnly
*Severity*::
INFORMATIONAL
*Description*::
This message occurs when a volume or aggregate is mounted as read-only.
*Corrective Action*::
(None).
*Syslog Message*::
Mounting vol/aggr '%s' readonly due to 'online -ro' command.
*Parameters*::
*name* (STRING): Name of the volume or aggregate.
